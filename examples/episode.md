# EPISODE dry run — The last lantern

**User:** `A knight carries the last lantern across a realm where every star is going out.`

**Route:** EPISODE. Produce one board at a time.

## Board 1 — Hook + Setup

Open on a star blinking out above the faceless knight, already walking with one amber lantern. Establish sparse road, distant ruined tower, and objective without a prolonged establishing shot.

## Board 2 — Escalation

Begin from Board 1's exact end state. Wind and spectral creatures pressure the lantern; the knight shelters it with the same gauntlet and continues toward the same tower.

## Board 3 — Major Turn

The lantern extinguishes at the tower threshold. A faint star-shaped reflection remains on the same breastplate plane, revealing the armor itself has carried the last light.

## Board 4 — Payoff / Resolution

The knight raises the same block gauntlet; the plate reflection transfers to the tower beacon and stars return one by one. End quietly on the unchanged knight watching the sky.

**Grounding router:** *Summoner* for raw asset construction, *Legacy of Kain: Defiance* for spectral silhouette/cool darkness, and *Shadow of the Colossus* for sky exposure/negative space only. One source-bound amber/cool-white bloom family. No Board 5 is needed.

**Approval rule:** Stop after each board and wait. Lock the lantern, tower geography, armor, weather, bloom, and action state.
