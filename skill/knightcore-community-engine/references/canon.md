# Anchor Knight canon

Read this for every creation that uses the default Knightcore archetype.

## Status and authority

The bundled `../assets/knightcore-anchor-reference.jpeg` is approved and locked. Its SHA-256 is:

```text
321195c5886547227c5d2618bdcb6da8409f82157d49f908dd270a908938a3db
```

It is a production reference for a recognizable faceless chrome knight—not a named mascot, official new character, monarch, or lore declaration. Never alter the bundled file.

## Archetype lock

Preserve by default:

- adult humanoid proportions built from sparse, visibly faceted geometry
- enclosed great helm with narrow black visor slit; no visible face
- simple perforation marks low on the helmet
- broad, low-detail silver breastplate and waist plate
- compact angular pauldrons and block forearm/gauntlet forms
- minimally separated or fused fingers
- simple cuisses, greaves, knee transitions, and angular sabatons
- painted dark chainmail tile at upper arms, hips, and knee gaps
- broad polygon highlight planes instead of smooth reflective metal
- crude projected/hard shadow behavior
- readable silhouette at thumbnail size

Optional: simple dark cape, longsword, shield, armor wear, one cool reflection/cloth accent, and era-appropriate guild variants. These additions may not increase the base mesh or material sophistication.

## Separate five kinds of truth

- **Archetype canon:** the locked model construction above
- **Community grammar:** Knightcore tone, motifs, and relatable premises
- **Rendering authority:** original-PS2 references assigned to narrow visual roles
- **Project continuity:** currently approved wardrobe, props, world, light, positions, damage, and action
- **Creative interpretation:** new scene-specific people, creatures, places, jokes, and events

An approved scene may change current pose, props, setting, or light. It cannot silently smooth, subdivide, redesign, or materially enrich the underlying Anchor Knight.

## Reject drift

Fail the image when the knight becomes a modern photoreal/PBR hero, ornate Soulslike warrior, superhero, sci-fi power suit, chrome robot, franchise-specific crusader, or celebrity-like exposed face. Also fail modeled chainmail, detailed finger joints, dense rivets/trim, layered filigree, true mirror reflection, or a modern studio-render backdrop.
