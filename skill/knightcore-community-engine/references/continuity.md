# Approval and continuity

Read this for approved visuals, sequences, storyboards, episodes, revisions, or repairs.

## Project ledger

Retain in the current project:

- selected mode, output format, aspect ratio, and target model
- selected style adapter and version, plus selected motion profile
- Anchor Knight or requested guild-member identity and armor variant
- latest approved image/storyboard and approval state
- wardrobe, cape, weapons, shields, prop ownership, creatures, and damage
- environment, geography, subject positions, screen direction, and spatial anchors
- light, palette, weather, bloom color family/intensity, and time of day
- active PS2 build when relevant, accepted screenshot set, reference-role map,
  and selected rendering contract
- shot order, rhythm roles, dominant motion channels, action state, episode
  board number, and unresolved causal state
- pre-state, change-only delta, and post-state for transformations or other
  state changes
- prompt limit, audio requirement, and prior repairs
- selected video creation route, H3 Max endpoint, reference order, and
  prompt-expansion mode when applicable

Reset only when the user starts a new idea, says `new project`, or explicitly changes authority.

## Locks after approval

An approved project image controls current pose, wardrobe, props, scene, light,
action, and project-specific style continuity. The bundled Anchor Knight still
controls the underlying default helmet, plate arrangement, painted-chainmail
placement, restrained complexity, and recognizable archetype. A selected style
adapter may translate those features into its medium but cannot replace them.

Progress action rather than resetting it. Preserve weapon/prop counts, handedness, cape state, armor damage, entrances/exits, and who owns each object. Maintain screen direction unless a motivated camera crossing is shown.

## Narrow repair

Use:

```text
LOCK:
[every correct layer]

CHANGE ONLY:
[one requested or diagnosed failure]

DO NOT CHANGE:
[identity, project state, composition, geography, active build,
lighting/bloom, action state, and all passing layers]
```

Repair in this order: archetype/identity; anatomy and prop fusion/count; underlying asset construction; project state; geography/action continuity; era rendering; bloom; decoration. Regenerate only the failed shot or board when practical. A repair may not silently smooth or enrich the knight.

For melee, reduce a failing shot to one readable strike and one attack path;
separate contact from follow-through when overlaps create duplicate limbs or
weapons. For a morphing transformation, restore exact pre- and post-states,
bridge them with environmental escalation and one brief impact or silhouette
insert, then hard-cut to the completed post-state.

If the camera feels stiff, add shot-scale contrast, one restrained push-in, a
brief detail insert, or a decisive cut. Do not add constant movement. If held
lines, shadows, grain, helmet, or armor crawl, stabilize those layers and
animate only the declared subject, camera, or effects channel.
