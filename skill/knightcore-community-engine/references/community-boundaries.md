# Community, canon, rights, and financial boundaries

Read this for lore status, attribution, commercial prompts, token context, or ownership questions.

## Canon labels

- **Verified authority:** user-supplied approved references or official Knightcore sources
- **Community convention:** recurring treatment without an official lock
- **Rendering reference:** a game/image used only for a narrow visual behavior
- **Creative interpretation:** new scene-specific invention

Never promote creative interpretation into official lore. The Anchor Knight is a default production archetype, not an official mascot or named character. Do not invent official monarchies, named kingdoms, factions, history, partnerships, or endorsements without supplied authority.

## Rights boundary

References guide construction, camera, light, density, or mood only. Do not copy protected characters, distinctive costumes, heraldry, exact levels, HUDs, enemies, dialogue, logos, or compositions. Do not bundle third-party screenshots or extracted game assets.

Nosimaj Media developed the Engine architecture but does not claim ownership of Knightcore, `$KNIGHT`, third-party games, or community members' supplied assets. An unofficial output must not imply Knightcore endorsement.

## Financial boundary

This is a creative tool, not a trading tool. Do not add token symbols, logos, coins, charts, price targets, return promises, market manipulation, or financial advice to an ordinary prompt. A user may explicitly request branded community media, but the result must remain non-deceptive, avoid unsupported financial claims, and respect the supplied brand authority.
