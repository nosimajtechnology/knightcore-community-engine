# Community guide

## A strong idea is simple

Give the Engine one feeling or situation, one action, and one consequence. A sentence is enough. The Engine handles the Anchor Knight, PS2 references, bloom, camera, and repair.

Knightcore can be ordinary, sincere, dreamy, romantic, tired, absurd, or spectacular. You do not need to add combat, a king, a quest, or a sword to every scene.

The default is luminous medieval dreamcore and sincere PS2 wonder. Dark fantasy remains available when you ask for it.

## Approve in stages

For a cinematic or episode, judge the first image before expanding it. Reply `Approved` when the knight, setting, and fidelity are right. A later correction should name only the failed layer so the Engine preserves everything else.

## Keep remix status clear

The Anchor Knight is a default archetype, not a new official mascot. New places, guild members, creatures, jokes, and story events are community interpretations unless an official source establishes them.

Do not imply that Nosimaj owns Knightcore or that an unofficial scene is endorsed. Do not copy a game character, level, HUD, logo, or exact composition merely because that game supplied rendering reference.

## Keep finance out of ordinary art

The Engine does not automatically add `$KNIGHT`, token logos, prices, charts, or promises. If you intentionally make community-branded media, keep it non-deceptive and avoid financial advice or unsupported claims.
